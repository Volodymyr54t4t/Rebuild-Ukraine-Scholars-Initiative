Motivation

Why I build, why I study, and why it matters.

Dear Selection Committee,

My name is Volodymyr Chyzhevskyi, and I am a first-year Software Engineering student at Zhytomyr Polytechnic State University. From an early age I was drawn to how technology works — not just to use it, but to understand and build it.

Through competitions, research, hackathons and STEM activities, I learned that engineering is most meaningful when it solves real problems. Winning first place at Hack The FICT with the HelioCore AI project confirmed that I want to work at the intersection of software, energy and intelligent systems.

The Rebuild Ukraine Scholars Initiative represents exactly the future I want to be part of: using knowledge and technology to rebuild my country — its infrastructure, its industry and its opportunities for young people.

With this scholarship, I will keep developing as an engineer and researcher, contribute to open and impactful projects, and help create technology that keeps talent, jobs and hope in Ukraine.

Sincerely,
Volodymyr Chyzhevskyi
