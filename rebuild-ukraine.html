Rebuild Ukraine

Why I am part of the Scholars Initiative — and how engineering serves reconstruction.

The Rebuild Ukraine Scholars Initiative invests in a generation of young engineers, researchers and builders who will help rebuild the country — not only its infrastructure, but its knowledge, industry and future.

As a Software Engineering student, I see technology as one of the most powerful tools for reconstruction: resilient energy systems, smart infrastructure, modern education, and startups that create jobs and keep talent at home.

How I Contribute

Energy & IoT

Building intelligent, efficient energy and monitoring systems — like HelioCore AI — for a more resilient grid.

Education & STEM

Sharing knowledge through STEM activities and research so more young Ukrainians can enter technology.

Startups & Jobs

Turning research into startups that create local opportunities and keep engineering talent in Ukraine.
